# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn & Retention Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge in the telecom industry due to intense competition and high customer acquisition costs. This project analyzes customer demographics, service usage, contract characteristics, tenure, and billing behavior to identify patterns associated with customer churn.

The analysis focuses on understanding the key factors influencing customer retention and generating actionable business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement

Customer churn is a major challenge in the telecom industry, where customers can easily switch between competing service providers. Factors such as contract type, pricing, tenure, service usage, and customer support can influence their decision to leave, resulting in revenue loss and increased customer acquisition costs.

Understanding customer behavior and identifying the factors associated with churn is therefore essential for improving customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective is to analyze customer data using numerical and categorical features to identify churn patterns, understand key factors influencing customer retention, and generate actionable business insights.

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis

The project analyzes various aspects of customer behavior, including:

* 👤 Customer demographics
* 📅 Customer tenure
* 📄 Contract type
* 📞 Phone services
* 🌐 Internet services
* 🔐 Online security and backup services
* 🛠️ Technical support
* 💳 Payment methods
* 💰 Monthly and total charges
* 📈 Customer churn patterns

---

## 💡 Key Findings

* 📅 Customers with **shorter tenure** show a higher likelihood of churn.
* 📄 **Month-to-month contract** customers have a higher churn rate compared with customers on longer-term contracts.
* 💰 Customers with **higher monthly charges** show increased churn tendencies.
* 🌐 Certain **internet service types** are associated with higher churn rates.
* 💳 Certain **payment methods** show higher customer churn.
* 🛠️ Customers without additional services such as **OnlineSecurity and TechSupport** show different churn patterns.
* 👥 Customer characteristics such as **SeniorCitizen, Partner, and Dependents** also show differences in churn behavior.
