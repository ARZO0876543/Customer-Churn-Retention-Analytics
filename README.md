# Telecom Customer Churn & Retention Analytics


## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge in the telecom industry due to intense competition and high customer acquisition costs. This project analyzes customer demographics, service usage, contract characteristics, tenure, and billing behavior to identify patterns associated with customer churn.

The analysis focuses on understanding the key factors influencing customer retention, developing business-focused KPIs, and generating actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Aim

The objective is to analyze customer data using numerical and categorical features to identify churn patterns, understand key factors influencing customer retention, and generate actionable business insights.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate
- Retention Rate
- Average Monthly Charges
- Average Tenure
- Total Revenue
- Churn by Contract Type
- Churn by Internet Service
- Churn by Payment Method

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis

The project analyzes:

- Customer demographics
- Customer tenure
- Contract type
- Internet and phone services
- Online security and technical support
- Payment methods
- Monthly and total charges
- Customer churn patterns

---

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis identifies customer segments with higher churn risk and highlights the factors associated with customer attrition.

These insights can help telecom businesses:

- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Improve customer retention strategies
- Optimize service offerings
- Improve customer support
- Develop targeted retention campaigns

---

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to visualize:

- Customer and churn KPIs
- Churn distribution
- Customer segmentation
- Contract-wise churn
- Service-wise churn
- Tenure analysis
- Revenue and billing patterns
